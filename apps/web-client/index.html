Roam Desk

Pair this browser with your PC once. On the PC, open the helper dashboard, choose Pair a device, and copy the pairing text.

The pairing text works once and expires after 5 minutes. This browser keeps a key that can't be copied out of it; unpair it from the dashboard or from Settings here.
